## Personas - Reddit

## Persona 1

Name: William Wallstedt

Age: 44

Title: one of few moderators for a large swedish subreddit 

Goal: To efficiently moderate discussions while maintaining a balance between healthy debate and freedom of speech.

Challenges: To manually review long comment sections without limiting users' right to express themselves.

Behaviour: Barely uses any existing tools from Reddit and has limited time to moderate the subreddit. Moderates mainly in short sessions during evenings.

## Persona 2

Name: Olle Karlsson

Age: 27

Title: daily active reddit user in a political swedish subreddit

Goal: to consume news and politics and have constructive discussions with other users.

Challenges: To navigate through unserious comment sections with bots.

Behaviour: Visits reddit frequently during the day and posts several times a week.

## Persona 3

Name: Frida Reperbahn 

Age: 22

Title: moderator for a small swedish political subreddit

Goal: make sure the subreddit is an open and comfortable community where everyone can express their opinions without facing hate or harassment. 

Challenges: As a single moderator for a subreddit Frida finds it hard to keep up with the posts and comments posted in the subreddit. Frida has limited time to be active on Reddit and therefore sometimes misses when discussions go over the limit.

Behaviour: Frida checks the subreddit once a day after work and remove posts and comments that break the rules of the subreddit.

## Persona 4

Name: Johan Johansson

Age: 18

Title: Infrequent Reddit user.

Goal: To visit subreddits when other sources lack information.

Challenges: Johan thinks a lot of information on Reddit is very extreme which makes him uncomfortable using the platform.

Behaviour: Visits Reddit a few times a month.
