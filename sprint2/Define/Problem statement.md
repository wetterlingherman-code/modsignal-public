## Problem statement

## Problem:

A Reddit moderator needs a more efficient way to navigate large subreddits and distinguish between constructive and harmful comments, because the high volume of content makes it difficult to moderate effectively and can negatively impact the community.

## Background:

The Reddit platform is a place of large amounts of heated discussions between users with different opinions. A subreddit has one or more moderators. Several moderators have complained about not having enough time to detect which comments are useful or not. Several users find the comment sections chaotic and non constructive.

## Details:

Large subreddits generate thousands of comments daily. Many comments are long and hard for moderators to manually review. Many discussions develop into echo chambers, where users primarily engage with opinions that reinforce their existing beliefs. The existing tools mostly focus on detecting harmful words which can be problematic in certain discussions, especially in subreddits for sensitive topics (e.g death sentence) where those words are used.

## Impact:

Subreddits without proper moderation can descend into environments characterized by harassment, hate speech and bullying. In absence of diverse moderation communities can become narrow minded where ideas are repeated and validated within the community, which can create echo chambers.
