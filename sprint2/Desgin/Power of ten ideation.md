## Kärnidé 1:

## How might we help moderators moderate on large scale subreddits without limiting freedom of speech/expression.

## 1

Automated flagging system based on keywords/phrases that might be controversial or break the rules of the subreddit

## 2

Ranking system based on user history (flagged comments, amount of reports)

## 3

Word detection system that helps moderators make easier decisions fast on longer comments

## 4

Some sort of protecting layer against bots before joining a subreddit (Captcha)

## 5

Bot detector that checks for “usual” bot language that might be used to create polarization 

## 6

A dashboard for the moderator with a users engagement in “extreme” subreddits. Could be harmful as everyone judges “extreme” differently. 

## Kärnidé 2: 

How might we help reddit users navigate through controversial topics without limiting their experience

## 1 
“Red light” system which indicates a “flair” through a red/yellow/green light system based on the comments use of language (calculated based on “toxic” terms which might create polarizations)

## 2

An AI detected point system (0-100) that shows how “polarised and safe” a post is. Upwards to 100, the AI detects a lot of slurs, personal attacks and so on. Near 1, the AI detects near to no slurs and estimates that the comment section is productive.

## 3

Ai Fact checking that flags desinformation or opinions for readers (exempelvis, “det här är en åsikt vs det här är baserat på fakta”)

## 4

Tool that blurs posts that are considered Controversial (typ som google bilder, där man kan trycka på visa bild, kanske inte tas emot väl av användare)

## 5

Chrome plugin that notifies users when navigating a well known subreddit for polarization/echo chambers etc
