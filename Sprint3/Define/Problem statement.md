#

## Problem:
A Reddit moderator needs a way to navigate through polarized discussions and encourage constructive dialogue, because the large scale of comments make it difficult to prevent harmful behavior and echo chambers.

## Background:
The Reddit platform is a place where large amounts of heated discussions between users with different opinions. Each subreddit has one or more moderators. Many moderators have complained about not having enough time to detect which comments are constructive or not. Many comments are harmful and made by bots. Several users find the comment sections chaotic and non constructive that only leads to unwanted polarization. 

## Details:
Large subreddits generate thousands of comments daily. Many comments are long and hard for moderators to manually review. Many discussions develop into echo chambers, where users primarily engage with opinions that reinforce their existing beliefs. The existing tools mostly focus on detecting harmful words which can be problematic in certain discussions, especially in subreddits for sensitive topics (e.g death sentence) where the context is crucial. 

## Impact:
Subreddits without proper moderation can descend into environments characterized by harassment, hate speech and bullying. On the other hand, in the absence of diverse perspectives, communities risk becoming insular, where ideas are repeatedly reinforced within the group, contributing to the formation of echo chambers.
