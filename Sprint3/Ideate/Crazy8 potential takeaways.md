## Crazy 8’s potential functions/takeaways:
- A graph over time that shows a certain user’s reports 
- A potential risk score that includes a combination of metrics (harmful words, threats, curse words, long comments etc)
- Keep comments anonymous to avoid confirmation bias within moderators
- A tool that shows bot-like comments
- A “today’s comments to watch” with prioritization where a moderator quickly can see what needs to be done. 
- A potential chat within moderators
- A transparency comment that shows why a moderator has blocked or deleted a comment
