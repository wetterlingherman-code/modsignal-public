Here is a short explanation of each picture created in the crazy 8 session, these were then prioritized by each group member according to the MoSCoW system.
- M = Must have
- S = Should have
- C = Could have
- W = Won't have

1. A “report card” that shows the amount of harmful words, threats etc in a comment made by a user. S S S S
2. An overview of a certain user’s comments which include amount of harmful words, threats etc for the specific comment S S S S M
3. A moderator chat where moderators can discuss a certain user’s behaviour C C C C W
4. A graph that shows amount of reports on a specific user. C S C C C
5. A trash/escalate/accept button that shows whether a moderator wants to keep a comment or not W S S C W
6. A combined metric that says something like “potential risk score” where escalations per user, harmful words, threats, curse words, long sentences etc are combined to a number (0-100) and helps the moderator. Could also be for comment sections. S S C M
