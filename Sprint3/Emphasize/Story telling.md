## Storytelling 
David is a moderator for a political subreddit with over 100,000 members. He opens his phone one morning and discovers that a post has exploded overnight, with thousands of new comments waiting for him.

Scrolling through the thread, he sees a flood of replies. Some are straight-up ignorant, full of foul language. Others are just personal attacks dressed up as arguments. And then there are the tricky ones, comments where it's hard to tell if they're actually relevant to the discussion or just noise pulling the conversation off track.

David has two options. He can spend his entire morning reading every single comment, or he can give up and lock the thread, silencing the constructive voices along with the toxic ones.

This is where our tool comes in.
Instead of forcing David to read every comment, our tool gives him a clear overview of the thread at a glance. Problematic comments are flagged. Personal attacks are surfaced. Off-topic noise is filtered out of the way. The comments that actually matter, the ones driving real discussion, are easy to find.

Now David can make decisions in minutes instead of hours. He keeps the thread alive, removes what needs to be removed, and moves on with his day.
That's the value we're building toward. Not replacing moderators, but giving them the tools they need to handle the scale of modern online discussions.
